1. 这是什么

GC VAULT（以下简称"本应用"）是一个个人使用的加密保险箱网页应用，网址 https://vault.xuts.net/。你输入的"名称 / 值"会在你自己的浏览器里用你的主密钥加密后，保存成 .xgcv 文件，存放在你自己的设备或你自己的 Google Drive 里。

2. 我们不收集什么

  • 你的主密钥和保险箱里的明文内容，从不离开你的浏览器，不会发送给本应用的任何服务器，也不会写入日志。
  • 本应用没有统计分析、广告、第三方跟踪脚本，也不出售、出租或向任何第三方分享任何数据。
  • 本应用没有用户账号系统，不收集姓名、邮箱、电话、位置等个人信息。

3. Google 用户数据

如果你选择使用 Google Drive 功能，本应用会请求且只请求一个权限：https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file。

  • 这个权限只允许本应用访问由本应用自己创建的文件（保存在 Drive 里名为 gc_vault2 的目录中），看不到也不会读取你 Drive 里的其他任何文件。
  • 访问这些文件的唯一目的：按你的操作列出、打开、保存你的加密保险箱文件。文件内容在上传前已经在你的浏览器里加密，Google 和本应用的服务端都读不到明文。
  • 本应用对从 Google API 获得的信息的使用和转移，遵守 Google API 服务用户数据政策，包括其中的"有限使用"要求。
  • 本应用不会把 Google 用户数据用于广告，不会让人工阅读这些数据，也不会用于训练任何模型。

4. 登录是怎么处理的

  • 登录由 Google 完成。为了让你"登录一次长期有效"，本应用使用一个很小的中转程序（运行在 Cloudflare Workers 上，地址 https://vault.xuts.net/oauth/*），它替浏览器向 Google 换取和刷新访问令牌。
  • 长期有效的"刷新令牌"加密后保存在 Cloudflare 的 KV 存储里，并只和一个随机的会话编号关联；你的浏览器里只有一个 HttpOnly 的 Cookie（__Host-ztsid），网页脚本读不到它。
  • 网页拿到的访问令牌有效期只有 1 小时，只保存在页面内存中，不写入浏览器存储。
  • 这个中转程序只处理登录令牌；它看不到你的保险箱文件，也看不到你的主密钥。会话 180 天不使用会自动失效。

5. 保存在你浏览器里的内容

  • 界面语言、闲置超时秒数、"曾经登录过 Drive"的提示标记（不含任何凭证）。
  • "最近打开的文件"：最多 16 条文件名和来源（本机 / Drive），以及电脑上本机文件的"文件句柄"（存在 IndexedDB 里，只是一个指向文件的引用，不含文件内容）。用于入口页的快捷图标，每个图标上可以点 × 移除；不含密钥、密文或文件内容。
  • 用于离线打开的应用文件缓存（Service Worker），不含你的保险箱数据。
  • 应用不会在浏览器里保存未加密的保险箱内容；闲置超时或你点"关闭"后，内存中的内容会被清除。

6. 保存期限与删除

  • 在 Drive 窗口点"退出登录"：本应用会向 Google 撤销令牌，并立即删除保存的会话。
  • 你也可以随时在 Google 账号的"第三方应用与服务"里删除本应用的访问权限；之后刷新令牌立即失效。
  • 你的保险箱文件由你自己保管：可以直接在 Drive 或本机删除。本应用的服务端不保存这些文件。
  • 清除浏览器的网站数据，即可删除本应用在你浏览器里保存的全部内容。

7. 安全

加密使用浏览器内置的 Web Crypto（PBKDF2-SHA256 派生密钥，AES-256-GCM 加密）。所有页面通过 HTTPS 提供。主密钥一旦丢失，保险箱无法恢复（本应用没有任何后门或找回机制）。

8. 浏览器扩展（GC VAULT Quick Save）

如果你安装了配套的浏览器扩展 GC VAULT Quick Save，请注意以下几点：

  • 读什么、什么时候读：只在你按快捷键、点右键菜单或点工具栏图标的那一刻，读取你当前页面里正在使用的输入框（或你选中的文字）的内容，以及该网站的域名。它不会在后台监视你的输入，也不会读取其他网页。
  • 暂存：读到的内容只暂存在浏览器内存里（chrome.storage.session），不写入磁盘，关闭浏览器即清除，最长保留 10 分钟。
  • 去哪里：内容交给扩展自己打开的小窗里的 GC VAULT（https://vault.xuts.net/），由它在你的浏览器里加密后保存到你自己选择的位置（你的设备或你自己的 Google Drive）。在你点"确定"之前，内容不会加入保险箱。
  • 不发送给任何人：扩展不会把任何内容发送给我们或任何第三方服务器；没有统计、广告或跟踪；不接触你的主密钥；没有账号。
  • 权限用途：activeTab 和 scripting 只用于在你触发的那一刻读取当前页面；contextMenus 用于右键菜单；storage 用于上面说的内存暂存；在 vault.xuts.net 上运行的内容脚本用于把内容交给小窗里的网页应用。扩展不申请读取所有网站的权限，也没有可选的网站权限。
  • 有限使用：扩展对从网页上读到的内容的使用，只限于你要求的功能（把这条内容保存到你的保险箱）：不出售、不用于广告、不用于与该功能无关的目的，也不会让人工阅读。
  • 卸载扩展即可停止；扩展暂存的内容随浏览器关闭而清除。

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

1. What this is

GC VAULT (the "App") is a personal, encrypted vault web app at https://vault.xuts.net/. The name/value pairs you enter are encrypted in your own browser with your master key and saved as .xgcv files on your own device or in your own Google Drive.

2. What we do not collect

  • Your master key and the plaintext contents of your vault never leave your browser. They are not sent to any server operated for the App and are not logged.
  • The App has no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking scripts, and does not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.
  • The App has no user account system and does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, or other personal information.

3. Google user data

If you choose to use the Google Drive feature, the App requests a single permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file.

  • This permission only lets the App access files created by the App itself (kept in a Drive folder named gc_vault2). It cannot see or read any other file in your Drive.
  • The only purpose is to list, open and save your encrypted vault files when you ask it to. File contents are encrypted in your browser before upload, so neither Google nor the App's server side can read the plaintext.
  • The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • The App does not use Google user data for advertising, does not allow humans to read it, and does not use it to train any model.

4. How sign-in is handled

  • Sign-in is performed by Google. To let you sign in once and stay signed in, the App uses a very small relay (running on Cloudflare Workers at https://vault.xuts.net/oauth/*) that exchanges and refreshes access tokens with Google on behalf of your browser.
  • The long-lived refresh token is stored encrypted in Cloudflare KV, associated only with a random session identifier. Your browser holds only an HttpOnly cookie (__Host-ztsid) that page scripts cannot read.
  • The access token the page receives lasts only one hour and is kept in page memory only — it is not written to browser storage.
  • The relay handles sign-in tokens only; it cannot see your vault files or your master key. A session that is unused for 180 days expires automatically.

5. What is stored in your browser

  • Interface language, idle timeout seconds, and a flag that you have signed in to Drive before (it contains no credentials).
  • "Recent files": up to 16 file names with their source (this device / Drive), plus, on desktop, a file handle for each local file (kept in IndexedDB; it is only a reference to the file, not its contents). It powers the quick-open icons on the start page, each of which can be removed with its × button. It contains no key, ciphertext or file contents.
  • A cache of the App's own files for offline start-up (Service Worker); it contains no vault data.
  • The App does not store unencrypted vault contents in browser storage; in-memory contents are cleared on idle timeout or when you click "Close".

6. Retention and deletion

  • Click "Sign out" in the Drive window: the App revokes the token with Google and immediately deletes the stored session.
  • You can also remove the App's access at any time in your Google Account under third-party apps and services; the refresh token stops working immediately.
  • Your vault files are yours to manage — delete them in Drive or on your device. The App's server side does not keep copies.
  • Clearing the site's data in your browser removes everything the App stored there.

7. Security

Encryption uses the browser's built-in Web Crypto (PBKDF2-SHA256 key derivation, AES-256-GCM). All pages are served over HTTPS. If you lose your master key the vault cannot be recovered — there is no back door or recovery mechanism.

8. Browser extension (GC VAULT Quick Save)

If you install the companion browser extension GC VAULT Quick Save, please note:

  • What it reads and when: only at the moment you press the shortcut, choose the context-menu item or click the toolbar icon, it reads the contents of the field you are using on the current page (or the text you selected) and the site's domain. It does not watch your typing in the background and does not read other pages.
  • Temporary storage: what it reads is kept only in browser memory (chrome.storage.session); it is never written to disk, is cleared when you close the browser, and is kept for at most 10 minutes.
  • Where it goes: it is handed to GC VAULT in a small window the extension opens (https://vault.xuts.net/), which encrypts it in your browser and saves it where you choose (your device or your own Google Drive). Nothing is added to your vault until you click OK.
  • Sent to no one: the extension does not send any content to us or to any third-party server; there is no analytics, advertising or tracking; it never touches your master key; there is no account.
  • Why each permission: activeTab and scripting only to read the current page at the moment you trigger it; contextMenus for the right-click item; storage for the in-memory hand-over above; the content script that runs on vault.xuts.net hands the content to the web app in the small window. The extension does not ask for access to all sites and has no optional site permissions.
  • Limited Use: the extension uses content read from web pages only for the feature you asked for (saving that content into your vault): it does not sell it, use it for advertising or for any purpose unrelated to that feature, and no human reads it.
  • Uninstall the extension to stop; anything it holds in memory is cleared when the browser closes.
